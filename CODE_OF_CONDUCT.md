# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate of different experience levels.
Focus feedback on code, evidence and observable behavior. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project
spaces. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation
for repeated or serious violations. Maintainers are held to the same standard.
Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub; do not
publish another person's private information. Reports will be handled discreetly.
